OUR CLASSES

Choose the class that meets you where you are.

A refined Pilates studio where intentional movement flows, built to strengthen your core and calm your mind.

All Levels

Reformer Pilates

Build strength, control, and alignment through guided full-body movement on the reformer. Our instructors offer modifications and challenges to suit every body.

Core Focus

Mat Pilates

Low impact workout performed on a mat using your body weight to build core strength, stability, and control. It focuses on precise movements and breathwork to improve posture, flexibility, and overall body alignment. Each session leaves you feeling stronger, more centered, and refreshed.

Mobility

Stretch & Restore

A Gentle, recovery focused class designed to release tension, improve flexibility, and support muscle recovery. Through slow, guided stretches and mindful breathing, it helps reduce stiffness and enhance overall mobility. It leaves you feeling relaxed, rebalanced, and deeply refreshed.

Personal

Private Sessions

One-on-one support tailored to your goals, pace, and movement needs.
